How I work

Research depth. Product judgment. Build fluency.

The through-line is not a particular tool. It is reducing uncertainty — first about the user, then about the product decision, then about whether the design actually works.

01

Find the behavioral bottleneck

Frame what someone is trying to understand or do, then identify the moment where the system makes that harder.

02

Make a concrete product bet

Translate the problem into an interaction model, prototype, or system — not just a list of recommendations.

03

Define the evidence

Decide what success would look like before claiming it: task completion, confidence, behavior, reliability, or another measurable signal.

About

I like the moment when a fuzzy human problem becomes a testable product decision.

Why this mix?

Cognitive science taught me to be skeptical of intuition about behavior. Product design gives me a way to turn that curiosity into something people can actually use. Code keeps the work honest by forcing decisions about states, constraints, and failure modes.
